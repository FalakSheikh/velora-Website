    Common questions

    How does the AI Wardrobe Scanner work?

    Upload one photo of your wardrobe. Velora finds each piece and sorts it by category, colour and style. If it gets something wrong, open the piece in My Wardrobe and edit it.

    Why do I only scan once?

    Every piece is saved to your account as its own item, so you never upload the same clothes again.

    Is my wardrobe private?

    Yes. Only you can see your wardrobe, outfits, chats and photos. The only things other members can see are the posts you choose to share in Community.

    How does Velora choose my outfits?

    From your own wardrobe, the occasion you pick, and the colours and styles you like. The more you save and wear, the better it fits you.

    What does Wore it do?

    It counts how often you wear a piece, so Insights can show what you use most and what you have forgotten.

    Can I change my email?

    Not yet. Your email is your login, so it stays the same.

    How do I download or delete my data?

    Open Privacy and security in Settings. You can download everything or delete your account there.

    What Velora is

    Velora knows the clothes you already own and helps you decide what to wear. It puts together complete looks with shoes, bag, scarf and accessories, for Pakistani and Western fashion, for every day and every occasion.

    What you can do

    • Scan your whole wardrobe from one photo
    • Get a complete look for today, your occasion and the weather
    • Plan your week in a minute
    • Chat with your stylist in the tone you like
    • Try looks on a model or on your own photo
    • See what you wear most in Insights
    • Save the looks and trends you love
    • Share your style with the community

    Our promise

    Your wardrobe, outfits, chats and photos are private to you. You choose what to share, and you can download or delete everything any time from Privacy and security.

    Community Rules
    1. Be kind. Speak to others the way you would like to be spoken to.
    2. Share your own style. Do not post other people's photos without their permission.
    3. No hate or shaming. No bullying, harassment, or comments about anyone's body.
    4. No spam. No adverts, links to sell things, or copied posts.
    5. Keep it fashion-friendly. No nudity or anything explicit.
    6. Help us keep it safe. Use Report on any post that breaks these rules. Our team can remove posts and members who do not follow them.
    How Velora handles your data

    Your wardrobe, outfits, chats and uploaded photos are private to your account. Community posts are visible to other members. Your try-on photo is used only to show you your looks. You can download or delete your data any time in Settings.
